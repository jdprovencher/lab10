**Title:** “Jolly Because You Loved Them”  
**Style:** Classic cheerful Christmas pop with sleigh bells, warm strings, and a bright sing-along feel  

---

### Music Description

This Christmas song has a light, bouncy, family-friendly vibe, like Santa strolling through a snowy village with a sack full of gifts.  

- **Tempo:** Moderate to upbeat, around **110–120 BPM**  
- **Key:** Bright and cheerful, such as **G major** or **C major**  
- **Feel:** Jolly, warm, festive, and easy to sing along to  
- **Instruments:**  
  - Sleigh bells on every chorus  
  - Soft piano or celesta  
  - Warm acoustic guitar  
  - Light bass  
  - Strings or brass accents  
  - Handclaps in the chorus  
  - Gentle bells and glockenspiel for sparkle  

The verses are storytelling and cozy, while the chorus lifts into a singable, happy “ho ho ho” feel.

---

## Lyrics

### Verse 1
The fire’s burning low in the snowy night,  
The stars are shining down on a world so bright.  
Santa checked his list, then he smiled once more,  
He’d been watching over every door.  
With a twinkle in his eye and a jingle bell,  
He had a story that he wanted to tell.
